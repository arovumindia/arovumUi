Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Arovum India Private Limited respects the privacy of individuals who contact us, enquire about our products, or engage with our business. This policy outlines how we collect, use and protect personal information.

Information we collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, mobile number, email address, business details, and product requirements when you contact us through the website, WhatsApp, email, or other business communication channels.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, understand your needs, provide product information, and improve the quality of our service. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We retain contact information only for as long as necessary to provide the requested service, comply with applicable legal obligations, or improve our business processes.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. However, no system is completely risk-free, and we encourage customers to use secure communication channels when sharing sensitive information.
